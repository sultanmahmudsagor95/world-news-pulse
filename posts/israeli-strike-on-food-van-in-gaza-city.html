Israeli Strike on Food Van in Gaza City Kills Three as Ceasefire Toll Reaches 1,675

22 November 2026

Originally published on World News Pulse — read it on the main blog.

 

Displaced Palestinians in the Nuseirat area of central Gaza. (UNRWA photo — file photo)

Sunday, 22 November 2026 — An Israeli drone strike killed three people in a van delivering food to displaced Palestinians in Gaza City on Saturday, in the latest deadly incident since a United States-brokered ceasefire took effect in October 2025. Gaza's health ministry and witnesses said the van was distributing food aid, while Israel's military said the three men killed were members of the militant group Islamic Jihad. The charity whose logo was found inside the vehicle said the van was not its own.

The strike hit the van at around midday on Saturday in the Tel al-Hawa neighbourhood of south-western Gaza City, according to the Palestinian news agency WAFA and witnesses. A Reuters witness said the vehicle appeared to be carrying packaged food, and that papers inside it carried the logo of World Central Kitchen, a charity that distributes food in Gaza. Abu Zein al-Daya, who witnessed the attack, told Reuters: "It was just about to stop to distribute the food and drink to children when it was hit by a missile." Cooked rice from the van was scattered around the vehicle.

World Central Kitchen said the vehicle was not affiliated with the charity. It said the driver had previously worked for the charity, and the two other people in the van were not its workers.

Israel's military said on Saturday evening that it had struck three members of the military wing of Palestinian Islamic Jihad who were travelling in a vehicle "used to transport food and logistical supplies" to militants. It called claims by sources in Gaza that the dead were aid workers false. On Sunday, the military named the three men as Ahmad Badran Nimer Sawafiri, a company commander in Islamic Jihad's engineering unit, and Mustafa Yousef Mustafa Rifai and Khaled Od Fathi Na'im. It said one of them had previously worked for an international aid organisation and had been dismissed after Israel said he was a militant. Reuters said it could not independently verify the military's statement, and Islamic Jihad did not immediately comment.

A fourth Palestinian was killed by Israeli gunfire on Saturday in the Halawa displacement camp, east of Jabalia in northern Gaza, Al Jazeera reported, and three people were injured in a separate attack in al-Mawasi, west of Khan Younis. Gaza's Civil Defence said four people were killed across the territory on Saturday.

Gaza's health ministry said on Sunday that hospitals had received six people killed and 33 wounded over the previous 24 hours — four newly killed and two bodies recovered from rubble. The ministry said 1,675 Palestinians have now been killed and 6,095 wounded since the ceasefire began on 11 October 2025, and 851 bodies have been recovered from the rubble. Since the war began in October 2023, the ministry said, 74,742 Palestinians have been killed and 176,227 wounded. The ministry's figures are considered reliable by the United Nations.

Civil Defence crews also recovered the bodies and remains of 129 people on Saturday from 39 destroyed buildings in Nuseirat, az-Zawayda and Deir el-Balah in central Gaza. Rami al-Aidi, the Civil Defence director in central Gaza, said 233 people had been reported missing under the rubble of those buildings.

On Saturday, hundreds of displaced Palestinians protested in Deir el-Balah against the destruction of homes beyond the "Yellow Line", the line Israeli forces withdrew to under the ceasefire. Mohammed Musleh, mayor of the Maghazi municipality in Deir el-Balah, told Al Jazeera that more than 500 housing units had been destroyed in less than a month. Gaza's housing ministry says 350,000 families are homeless after Israeli attacks destroyed 80 percent of the enclave's homes. Israel now controls about two-thirds of Gaza.

The humanitarian situation is worsening as winter sets in. Flooding has devastated temporary tent housing, and the World Food Programme warned this week that Palestinians have been forced to burn rubbish to cook. The International Organization for Migration said more than 100,000 tents in Egypt cannot be delivered to Gaza because of Israeli conditions on their entry.

In the occupied West Bank, a Palestinian man was shot dead late on Friday by security guards from an Israeli settlement near Dura al-Qari, north of Ramallah, the Palestinian Health Ministry said. Hamza Masoud Salim Abdo, 42, was killed and another man detained; his body is being withheld by Israeli authorities. Al Jazeera reported that fewer than 3 percent of investigated settler attacks lead to a conviction.

The ceasefire took effect in October 2025 after two years of war that began with Hamas's attack on Israel on 7 October 2023, in which 1,200 people were killed and more than 250 taken hostage. It has reduced the scale of fighting but has not stopped deadly incidents, and Hamas has called for an urgent meeting of the ceasefire mediators after Saturday's strike.
